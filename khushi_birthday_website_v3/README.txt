KHUSHI'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — VERSION 3

WHAT'S NEW
- Starts with “Khushi, are you excited for your birthday?” and Yes / No buttons.
- The No button dodges when hovered.
- Four interactive balloons appear on the next screen.
- Each popped balloon is replaced in the SAME spot by its birthday wish; wishes do not appear as toast/notification popups.
- Once all four wishes are revealed, the Continue button appears.
- Separate screens for the rose bouquet, cake wish, personalized photo scrapbook, birthday letter, and final “I love you, Khushi!” screen.
- Includes all seven photos you uploaded (four original photos plus these three new photos).

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser. Keep the photos folder next to index.html.

PUBLISH ON GITHUB PAGES
Upload index.html and the entire photos folder to the root of your repository. In GitHub, open Settings > Pages and deploy from your main branch using /(root).
